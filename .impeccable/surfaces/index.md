# Monthly ledger surface

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A private monthly ledger that makes planned and actual money legible at a glance; it refuses the generic finance dashboard of floating statistic cards and decorative charts.

**OWN-WORLD:** Warm paper is the ground, near-black ink carries the balance sheet and rules, income uses deep green, and expense uses vermilion. Ruled rows, tabular numerals, restrained roundness, and WenKai display lettering make the interface recognizable without its copy.

**STORY:** The user opens the ledger, understands the current balance and this month's variance, records one monthly expected amount per category, then opens a category to inspect or add exact dated actual records. An expense paid with Huabei remains on its purchase date without reducing this month's balance, and reappears as next month's expected repayment. The user can leave knowing the data never left the device.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** The title and month sit above one dark balance sheet. Current balance dominates; expected remaining, actual remaining, and plan difference share its lower rule. All six monthly categories and only their totals follow in one ruled table above the fixed navigation, with the central plus as the primary action. Category detail is a separate dated ledger surface.

**FORM:** The chosen form is the seventh grounded direction, a Chinese household ledger translated into a compact phone sheet. Impeccable direction seed: `38ea137b`.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
